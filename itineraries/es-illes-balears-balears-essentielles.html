Majorca Road Trip: Serra de Tramuntana and Coves : 5 stops

Palma

🛏️ Suggested base : Palma
★★★★☆
1

La Seu Cathedral has overlooked the bay since the 13th century. Its nave rises to 45 meters. The 14-meter Gothic rose window is among the largest in the world.

⏱ 90 min
2

Almudaina Palace was built in the 14th century on the site of a former Arab alcazaba. The official residence of the King of Spain in Majorca, it houses the Chapel of Saint Anne and state rooms.

⏱ 75 min
3

Parc de la Mar stretches beneath the ramparts. Its artificial lake reflects La Seu in a photographic composition emblematic of Palma.

⏱ 45 min
4

Old town lanes wind between flower-filled patios and ochre facades. The quarter hides Baroque churches, noble houses, and shaded squares.

⏱ 90 min
5

Step through the doors of the Arab Baths, dating to the 10th century. Their barrel vaults and columns bear witness to Moorish occupation.

⏱ 45 min
6

Bellver Castle crowns a hill 3 kilometers away. This circular 14th-century fortress offers a panorama over the whole bay.

⏱ 90 min
7

Passeig del Born is Palma's elegant thoroughfare. Lined with plane trees and modernist facades, it connects Plaça de la Reina to the waterfront.

⏱ 60 min

Valldemosa

🛏️ Suggested base : Soller · 10 km
★★★★☆
1

Valldemossa Charterhouse was built in 1309 as a royal residence for the asthmatic son of Jaume II. Converted to a monastery in 1399, it hosted Chopin and George Sand during the winter of 1838–1839.

⏱ 90 min
2

You can visit Chopin and Sand's cells and see the authentic Pleyel piano. The walls preserve memories of the winter that inspired the Preludes and “A Winter in Majorca”.

⏱ 75 min
3

The monastic pharmacy dates to 1722. Considered one of Europe's best-preserved, it displays jars, alembics, and medicinal plants cultivated by the monks.

⏱ 60 min
4

The church's turquoise-green bell tower is visible from afar. Its azulejo tiles depict Saint Catalina Tomàs, Majorca's patron saint, born in Valldemossa in 1531.

⏱ 45 min
5

Cobbled lanes climb between stone houses with potted geraniums. Every facade displays a different azulejo illustrating the life of the beatified woman.

⏱ 75 min
6

The Charterhouse gardens cascade in terraces. Orange, lemon, and almond trees offer panoramic views over the verdant valley.

⏱ 60 min

Soller

🛏️ Suggested base : Soller
★★★★☆
1

The Sóller train has run with varnished wooden carriages since 1912. It traverses 27 kilometers of the Serra de Tramuntana between Palma and Sóller, passing through tunnels, over bridges, and around impossible curves.

⏱ 75 min
2

Modernist Sant Bartomeu Church was restored by Joan Rubió i Bellver, a disciple of Gaudí. Its neo-Gothic facade and colorful stained-glass windows dominate Plaça Constitució.

⏱ 60 min
3

The wooden tram links Sóller to the port in 30 minutes. It meanders between orange trees, lemon trees, and traditional houses for 5 kilometers.

⏱ 45 min
4

Port de Sóller curves around a protected bay. Its waterfront restaurants serve Sóller's red prawns and seafood paella.

⏱ 90 min
5

Can Prunera—a modernist museum—occupies a mansion built in 1911. Its Nolla ceramics, ironwork, and stained glass illustrate the wealth of citrus merchants.

⏱ 75 min
6

Can Prunera's gardens spread beneath palms and magnolias. An 18th-century pergola shades paved walkways and pools.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Boats depart for Sa Calobra and its vertiginous cliffs. The cove nestled at the base of a canyon offers turquoise waters and a pebble beach.

⏱ 180 min

The Port's crystalline waters invite snorkeling. Fish, starfish, and posidonia meadows populate the rocky seabed.

⏱ 90 min

The Sóller Valley has cultivated oranges and lemons since the 10th century. Orange sorbet is an unmissable specialty.

⏱ 30 min

The village of Fornalutx is nestled 10 minutes away. Ranked among Spain's most beautiful, its flower-filled lanes climb between stone houses.

⏱ 120 min

Alcudia

🛏️ Suggested base : Alcudia
★★★☆☆
1

Alcúdia's ramparts date to the 14th century. Stretching 1.5 kilometers with 26 towers, they once guarded the town from pirates. Access begins at Porta de Vila Roja.

⏱ 75 min
2

Porta de Xara—a monumental gateway—opens onto the old town. Its golden stones and semicircular arch exemplify Majorcan medieval architecture.

⏱ 30 min
3

The Roman site of Pollentia was founded in 123 BC. Remains include a theater, forum, and patrician houses from the ancient Balearic Roman capital.

⏱ 90 min
4

The Monographic Museum displays artifacts unearthed at Pollentia. Pottery, jewelry, coins, and sculptures trace 2,000 years of history.

⏱ 60 min
5

Sant Jaume Church rises at the heart of the old town. Its Baroque interior contrasts with the medieval cobbled lanes surrounding it.

⏱ 45 min
6

Alcúdia Beach stretches for 3.5 kilometers. Its fine sand, shallow waters, and Blue Flag status make it one of Majorca's most family-friendly.

⏱ 90 min
7

Port d'Alcúdia is the modern resort area. Its seafront promenade, restaurants, and marina bring the waterfront to life.

⏱ 75 min

Ibiza

🛏️ Suggested base : Ibiza
★★★★☆
1

Dalt Vila—a fortified citadel—has towered over Ibiza Town since the 16th century. UNESCO-listed, it features Renaissance ramparts, bastions, and cobbled lanes offering panoramic views of the harbor.

⏱ 120 min
2

Portal de ses Taules opens onto Dalt Vila. This monumental gateway flanked by Roman statues is the upper town's main entrance.

⏱ 30 min
3

Santa María Cathedral was erected in the 14th century atop the former mosque. Its Gothic character gained Baroque additions in the 18th century.

⏱ 60 min
4

The Archaeological Museum in Dalt Vila is closed, but the Puig des Molins necropolis museum, at the foot of the upper town, remains open. A UNESCO World Heritage site, the necropolis displays Phoenician, Punic and Roman remains, and a path winds across the hill riddled with rock-cut tombs.

⏱ 75 min
5

Ibiza's coves shine an unreal turquoise. Cala Comte, Cala d'Hort facing Es Vedrà and Cala Salada are among the most spectacular, each about thirty minutes' drive from Ibiza town.

⏱ 180 min
6

Eivissa harbor pulses day and night. Yachts, fishing boats, and Formentera ferries line the marina.

⏱ 60 min
7

Ses Salines Natural Park extends between Ibiza and Formentera. This UNESCO site harbors flamingos, traditional salt pans, and pristine beaches.

⏱ 90 min
