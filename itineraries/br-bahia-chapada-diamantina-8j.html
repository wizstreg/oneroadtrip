✨ Highlights▾
  • Vale do Pati, voted among the world's best treks: 3 days in the valleys, family homestays, no cell phone signal
  • Cachoeira da Fumaça, a free fall of 340–380 m, one of Brazil's highest waterfalls and the highest easily accessible one, named for the smoke-like effect of the spray
  • Morro do Pai Inácio, 1,120 m, the park's postcard view: a flat-topped plateau offering a 360° panorama at sunset
  • Poço Azul and Poço Encantado, underground lakes with fluorescent turquoise-blue waters (sunbeams from February to October at Poço Azul and April to September at Poço Encantado)
  • Igatu, "Brazil's Machu Picchu": stone village from the 19th-century garimpeiro era
  • Lençóis, historic diamond capital (1845), colorful colonial center classified as Brazilian national heritage
☀️ Weather▾

Best time: April to October (dry season), temperatures of 18–28°C, ideal for hiking. Avoid November–March: daily torrential rains, muddy and slippery trails. Altitude keeps tropical heat moderate year-round.

📋 Notes▾

Arrival and departure via Salvador (international flight via GRU or direct from Europe). Salvador → Lençóis by road: 415 km, 6 hours via the BR-324 and then the BR-242, much faster on a local Azul flight (1 hour, 3 weekly services). All excursions depart from Lençóis or Vale do Capão. Bring good walking shoes, a rain cape, a swimsuit and a dry bag. Climate: dry season from May to September, ideal for trekking. Sunbeams in the pools: February to October at Poço Azul and April to September at Poço Encantado (the midday sun striking the cave).

Chapada Diamantina - The Wild Brazil of Diamond Prospectors : 8 stops

Salvador

Suggested base : Salvador
★★★★☆
1

Arrive in Salvador de Bahia at Luís Eduardo Magalhães International Airport. Transfer to Pelourinho, 30 km, 45 min. Drop your bags at a pousada in the historic center. The afternoon is enough for a first taste of the Afro-Brazilian capital.

60 min
2

Pelourinho, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1985, was the central square of Brazil's first capital (1549–1763) from the 16th to 18th centuries. Its name recalls the pillory where slaves were whipped. The restored pastel facades, Igreja Nossa Senhora do Rosário dos Pretos and Praça Terreiro de Jesus form South America's most cohesive colonial ensemble.

90 min
3

The Igreja e Convento de São Francisco is closed for restoration: the interior and the cloister's azulejos cannot be visited. View its exterior façade from the Pelourinho. Built between 1708 and 1723 by the Franciscans, the church has an interior covered with 800 kilos of gold on gilded wood, currently inaccessible.

60 min
4

Take the Elevador Lacerda, an Art Deco elevator dating from 1873 that connects the upper and lower cities in 30 seconds, over a height difference of 72 m. It is an essential postcard view. The fare has been 1 BRL since it reopened in February 2025 (free with a SalvadorCard). At the bottom, visit Mercado Modelo for handicrafts (capoeira items, berimbaus and hammocks) and views of Baía de Todos os Santos.

45 min

Dine in Pelourinho at a Bahian restaurant. Signature dishes: moqueca de peixe (fish in coconut milk and dendê oil), acarajé (bean fritters fried in palm oil), vatapá. Uauá or Axego on Rua João de Deus are local favorites without being tourist traps.

120 min

Lencois

Suggested base : Lencois
★★★☆☆
1

Travel from Salvador to Lençóis. The Azul flight (3 flights a week, 1 hour) is strongly recommended, compared with 6 hours by bus via the BR-242. Horácio de Matos Airport is 20 km from central Lençóis, a 30 min transfer.

30 min
2

Lençóis, founded in 1845, was the capital of Brazil’s diamond boom. Its name comes from the white tents of garimpeiros seen from the hilltops, which resembled sheets (lençóis). It now has 10,000 inhabitants, but at its peak it was Bahia’s third-largest town. It has preserved its colonial street plan and colorful architectural heritage.

60 min
3

Stroll along Rua das Pedras and across Praça Otaviano Alves. Low houses with pastel facades, artisan workshops, former diamond merchants' residences. The old French vice-consulate (1873), now the Subprefeitura, recalls when the town dealt directly with European buyers. Stop by the Museu do Garimpo for context.

60 min
4

Spend the afternoon at Rio Serrano, a 20 min walk from the center. Natural pools carved into red sandstone, polished-rock slides and 2–3 m waterfalls. The water has a characteristic amber tint caused by plant tannins. A swim is ideal for recovering from the journey.

120 min
5

Continue upstream to Ribeirão do Meio, famous for its 10-meter natural waterslide. The rock smoothed by centuries of erosion lets you slide into a large pool that cushions your landing. Watch out for collisions with other swimmers on busy days. Municipal park entrance: 5 BRL.

90 min

90 min

Lencois

Suggested base : Lencois
★★★☆☆
1

A full day on the classic, unmissable “caves and Pai Inácio tour”. Leave Lençóis at 8:30 AM by van with an agency. The itinerary covers a total of 150 km by road and 6 km on foot over the course of the day. Return around 6:30 PM. About 200 BRL, all-inclusive with lunch.

30 min
2

First stop at Cachoeira do Mosquito, 40 min from Lençóis. A short walk (20 min one way) leads to a 25 m waterfall framed by ochre rocks. Swim in the natural pool below. Poço do Diabo requires a transfer by car followed by access on foot; it is not a continuation of the Cachoeira do Mosquito trail. Another waterfall in a more dramatic canyon, with a deep natural pool.

120 min
3

Gruta da Lapa Doce, 80 km to the west, is Brazil’s third-largest cave (42 km mapped, 850 m open to visitors). The main chamber rises to a height of 70 m. Stalactites, stalagmites, columns, calcite curtains and cathedral-like formations fill the cave. A guide and headlamp are compulsory for the 45 min visit.

90 min
4

Fazenda Pratinha, a neighboring private estate, is home to Gruta Azul (where blue light reflects off the crystal-clear water at 2:30 PM during the dry season) and Gruta da Pratinha, where you can float through 150 m of underground passages wearing a mask and snorkel (40 BRL supplement). The transparent waters contain small fish, while bats roost on the ceiling.

120 min
5

Finish at Morro do Pai Inácio. The 20 min ascent gains 250 m on a rocky trail. At an altitude of 1,120 m, the flat-topped plateau offers a 360° panorama of Serra do Sincorá, Morro do Camelo and Morrão. The low light at sunset sets the orange cliffs ablaze. It is the quintessential view of the Chapada.

75 min

Activities

Lunch is included in the tour at Fazenda Pratinha. A simple but generous buffet featuring food from Minas Gerais and Bahia (55–70 BRL). Wood-fired chicken, rice and beans, fresh salads. A welcome break halfway through the day.

75 min

Return to Lençóis around 6:30 PM. Have dinner at a restaurant in the center: Cozinha Aberta (whose chef worked in São Paulo and serves contemporary Chapada cuisine) or the classic Picanha na Praça for grilled meat. Enjoy a quiet evening and prepare your gear for Day 4.

90 min

Lencois

Suggested base : Lencois
★★★☆☆
1

The second major excursion takes in Poço Azul, Poço Encantado and the village of Igatu. It is a full day covering 286 km by road, with little walking but a great deal of driving. Leave at 8 AM and return at 5 PM. About 250 BRL including lunch and admission fees.

30 min
2

Poço Encantado, 150 km from Lençóis in the municipality of Itaetê, is a limestone cavity containing an underground lake 61 m deep. From April to September, between 10:30 AM and 1 PM, the sun's rays pass through a natural fissure and illuminate the water in fluorescent cobalt blue. Swimming is strictly prohibited to preserve the site.

60 min
3

Poço Azul, 20 km from Poço Encantado, features the same light phenomenon, with one major advantage: swimming is allowed. Float with a mask and snorkel, wearing the compulsory life jacket, in perfectly clear water at 22–24°C. The golden midday light transforms the experience into a golden underwater ballet.

75 min
4

On the way back, make a detour to Igatu, nicknamed ‘Brazil’s Machu Picchu’. This dry-stone village was built by garimpeiros in the 19th century. Ruined houses stand in the heart of the village, alongside a picturesque cemetery and the Museu do Garimpo, housed in a former miner’s home. The current population is 350.

90 min
5

In Igatu itself, the Ruínas do Garimpo, a 15 min walk from the center, form a completely abandoned former mining district. Dry-stone walls built without mortar, the remains of sorting workshops and cactus-lined paths create a silent, desert-like atmosphere reminiscent of a Western.

60 min

Activities

Lunch is included at one of the simple restaurants in the center of Igatu. Flor de Açucena serves typical home cooking: feijoada, galinha caipira and doces caseiros (guava, banana and umbu preserves). The stone interior has a warm atmosphere.

75 min

Return to Lençóis in the evening. On your last evening before the trek, have a light dinner, go to bed early and pack your backpack for 3 days. The agency generally provides a list: technical clothing, rain poncho, headlamp, 2–3 liters of water, sunglasses, sunscreen and insect repellent.

60 min

Vale Do Pati

Suggested base : Vale Do Pati
★★★★★
1

First day of trekking in Vale do Pati. Get up at 5 AM, then take a 1-hour 30-minute transfer to the village of Guiné, the main starting point. Briefing with the guide: safety, itinerary and environmental responsibility. No phone signal for the next 3 days.

60 min
2

Vale do Pati is regularly ranked among the world's 10 best treks. This isolated valley, formed by the Rio Pati between Palmeiras, Andaraí and Mucugê, can only be crossed on foot. Eleven families still live here today (400 at the height of the diamond-mining era), welcoming trekkers into their homes, which double as guesthouses, since 1997.

30 min
3

The trail begins via Aleixos with a sustained 1-hour climb and 300 m of elevation gain. At the top, the valley suddenly opens up in a sweeping panorama. Cross the Gerais do Rio Preto, a high plateau covered with candombás (endemic plants), fording small streams along the way. The terrain is rocky and the trail well marked.

180 min
4

Descend toward Mirante do Vale do Pati. The viewpoint looks down over the valley, framed by tabletop ridges. The villages of Igrejinha, Campo das Flores and Ruinha appear as white dots in the forest. It is easy to see why trekkers describe the site as a ‘hidden green utopia’.

45 min
5

The final descent to the hamlet of Igrejinha takes 1 hour 30 minutes and drops 500 m along a steep, rocky trail. Arrive in the late afternoon at the home of Seu Wilson and Maria, pioneers of hospitality in the valley since 1997. The room is simple, with a decent bed, a cold shower and solar power for 3 hours in the evening.

90 min

Activities

Have dinner with the family at Seu Wilson’s: rice, black beans, dried beef (carne-de-sol), vegetables from the garden and doce de leite for dessert. Cooking over a wood fire. You eat at a communal table with the other trekkers. Chatting, singing, guitar. The atmosphere is unique.

90 min

Early to bed (9 PM), a starry night without light pollution. The Milky Way is visible to the naked eye. Temperatures can drop below 15°C in June–July: wool clothing and a down sleeping bag are recommended. Roosters and birds provide a natural wake-up call at 6 AM.

60 min

Vale Do Pati

Suggested base : Vale Do Pati
★★★★★
1

The second day of the trek is the most spectacular. Leave at 7 AM with a picnic prepared by Maria. Head for Morro do Castelo, a 1,300 m rocky peak visible from throughout the valley. Pass through a short cave by headlamp before the final climb.

60 min
2

Morro do Castelo rises to 1,300 m. It offers a 360° panorama of the flat-topped ridges and the caatinga below; in clear weather, you can sometimes even see Cachoeira da Fumaça in the distance. It is one of the park’s finest viewpoints. Picnic at the summit: bread, cheese, rapadura and fruit.

90 min
3

Descend toward Cachoeirão (280 m). From the top of the canyon, look out over a sheer 260 m cliff. During the rainy season, more than 20 separate ribbons of water fall into the mist. The morning rainbows are renowned. Swimming is not possible here.

90 min
4

Return by an alternative trail via Cachoeira dos Funis and Poço da Árvore. The 50 m waterfall drops in three tiers into a deep pool where swimming is allowed. At 18°C, the water is bracing but refreshing after the exertion. Have lunch beside the pool.

120 min
5

Return to Seu Wilson’s in the late afternoon, a 1-hour 30-minute walk. Take a cold shower (or an icy one in June–July), nap and read. Some try the neighboring families’ stalls for fresh rapadura. The valley’s complete silence is one of the most memorable aspects of the stay.

120 min

Activities

A simple, hearty dinner: hot soup, galinha caipira (slow-cooked free-range chicken), rice, corn angu and cabbage salad. Many trekkers linger around the table: the absence of screens revives a forgotten sense of togetherness. Finish with a local artisanal cachaça as a digestif.

90 min

Stargaze at your leisure from the pousada courtyard. With no light pollution, the Milky Way stretches across the sky. Some guides lead impromptu sessions using a constellation chart (the Southern Cross, Scorpius and Centaurus). Bedtime is 9:30 PM.

60 min

Vale Do Capao

Suggested base : Lencois · 15 km
★★☆☆☆
1

On the final day of the trek, exit via Vale do Capão. Get up at 5:30 AM, have a full breakfast (pão de queijo, eggs, coffee and fruit), then say goodbye to Seu Wilson. It is a long day: 6 hours of walking, 700 m of cumulative elevation change and varied scenery.

60 min
2

Climb steadily for 2 hours toward Gerais do Vieira, a high plateau at 1,200–1,400 m. Low vegetation, candombás and endemic orchids. This former garimpeiro trail is recognizable by the piles of stones (mundéus) marking the route. A stop at the top to catch your breath and hydrate is essential.

150 min
3

Mirante da Fumaça, a natural overhang at an altitude of 1,040 m, is reached on a separate hike from Vale do Capão: it is not a stop on the direct exit from Vale do Pati via Gerais do Vieira. This viewpoint looks directly onto Cachoeira da Fumaça, which drops 340 to 380 m into a canyon below. Winds lift the spray and create the “smoke” effect that gives the waterfall its name. A feeling of weightlessness guaranteed.

60 min
4

The final descent to Vale do Capão takes 3 hours of walking. Pass through humid Atlantic forest with crystal-clear streams, orchids and bromeliads. Arrive in Caeté-Açu, known as Vale do Capão, an alternative, neo-hippie village established in the 1970s by residents in search of counterculture. It has 1,500 inhabitants and a unique atmosphere.

120 min

Activities

Have a late lunch at a vegetarian restaurant in Vale do Capão (Restaurante Capão or Santo Grão). The village has a large vegetarian and Hare Krishna community: palm soup, vegetable lasagna and fresh tropical juices. It is a radical change after 3 days of rustic cooking. Expect to pay 45–60 BRL.

75 min

Transfer by 4x4 from Vale do Capão to Lençóis in the late afternoon, a 1-hour 30-minute drive partly on unpaved roads. Arrive in Lençóis around 7 PM. Take a long shower, do some laundry and enjoy a quiet evening. Have a light dinner in the center and go to bed early. Your muscles will be sore the next morning.

150 min

Salvador

Suggested base : Salvador
★★★★☆
1

Enjoy a free morning in Lençóis. Depending on your flight time, you can take an easy walk to Poço Halley (20 min from the center, with peaceful swimming), visit the Museu do Garimpo if you did not have time on Day 2, or shop for handicrafts (semi-precious stone jewelry and banana and umbu sweets).

120 min
2

For a more active option, take a half-day excursion to Marimbus, a wetland nicknamed the Chapada’s ‘mini-Pantanal’. Travel by canoe along the Rio Roncador amid buritis (marsh palms), water lilies, jacaré caimans and waterbirds. Expect to pay 150 BRL per person for 4 hours, all-inclusive.

240 min
3

Transfer to Horácio de Matos Airport, 20 km, 30 min. Take the 1-hour Azul flight from Lençóis to Salvador in the middle of the afternoon. There are three flights a week, so be sure to check the operating day before booking. If there is no flight, take a Real Expresso bus from Lençóis to Salvador (6 hours, 120 BRL).

60 min
4

Arrive in Salvador in the evening. Depending on the departure time of your international flight, spend an extra night in the center or transfer directly to Luís Eduardo Magalhães Airport. Flights to Europe generally leave between 10 PM and 11 PM, either via GRU or direct.

60 min

Activities

If staying overnight in Salvador, enjoy a final Bahian dinner in the chic Rio Vermelho neighborhood (Boteco do França). Traditional moqueca, authentic dendê and a passion fruit caipirinha. One last taste of Bahia before returning home.

120 min

For one last artisanal cachaça from the Chapada to take home, choose Seleta or Bocoy, produced in Vale do Paraguaçu. They are sold at Mercado Municipal de Lençóis or at the airport. Expect to pay 60–100 BRL for an artisanal bottle. The bottle must go in checked baggage on international flights.

30 min
