☀️ Weather▾

Favor May, June, and September, with average highs of about 24 to 28 °C. Avoid visiting exposed sites during the hottest hours in July and August, when average highs are around 31 °C. For swimming, September offers sea temperatures around 23 °C, compared with about 19 °C in May.

📋 Notes▾

Plan for three full days, arriving in Chios the day before, then spending the nights in Pyrgi and Volissos. Avoid having your first or third day fall on a Tuesday, when the Mastic Museum and Nea Moni are closed.

Chios in 4 days: the mastic villages and Nea Moni : 9 stops

Chios

🛏️ Suggested base : Chios
★★★★☆
1

A fortress, but also a neighborhood where life goes on: Chios Castle is more than its ramparts. The fortifications bear the imprint of the Genoese, while the baths recall the Ottoman period. Start by exploring the narrow streets on foot, leaving your car outside the walls.

⏱ 60 min
2

Light filters through the small openings in the domes of the Ottoman baths at Chios Castle. This 18th-century hammam welcomed men and women at separate times; today, you can admire its restored architecture. Check the opening hours before exploring the rooms: this is a heritage visit, with no bathing.

⏱ 30 min

Pyrgi

🛏️ Suggested base : Pyrgi
★★★☆☆
1

Pyrgi turns its facades into pages of black-and-white patterns. These geometric decorations, called xysta, cover the houses of a medieval village tied to mastic cultivation. Move closer to examine the details, then step back into the square to compare the facades; avoid blocking the doors of residents’ homes.

⏱ 40 min
2

A short detour to Agioi Apostoloi, behind Pyrgi’s facades, is worthwhile. This Byzantine church follows the architectural model of Nea Moni, a connection that adds meaning to your later visit to the monastery. Look for the access alley near the square and check the opening hours before planning to visit the interior.

⏱ 20 min
3

You’ll understand the villages better after visiting the Chios Mastic Museum near Pyrgi. The exhibits connect the techniques used to cultivate the mastic tree, its resin, and the organization of the Mastichochoria; the trees extend the visit outdoors. A single tree thus sheds light on an entire landscape. Allow 1 hour for the museum, which is closed on Tuesdays.

⏱ 60 min
CarDrive to next stop: 6 km · 8min

Emporios

🛏️ Suggested base : Volissos · 34 km
★★★☆☆

Activities

If the sea is calm, treat yourself to a swim at Mavra Volia, near the harbor of Emporios. The black pebbles give the water its dark hue, and it gets deep quickly. Bring water shoes, keep an eye on children, and skip swimming if waves make entering the water difficult; leave the pebbles where they are.

⏱ 60 min
CarDrive to next stop: 13 km · 16min

Olympi

🛏️ Suggested base : Volissos · 26 km
★★★☆☆
1

Designed to defend the houses, Olympi’s vaults now form a covered route through the narrow streets. The central tower and the Trapeza, a medieval building with two levels, tell the story of this collective organization. Also look for Agia Paraskevi near the square; park outside the village and keep the passageways clear for residents.

⏱ 45 min
CarDrive to next stop: 4 km · 6min

Mesta

🛏️ Suggested base : Volissos · 25 km
★★★★☆
1

Mesta makes you work out which way to go, which is a good way to understand its defenses. The adjoining houses themselves formed the outer wall; covered lanes connect the village’s interior. Follow a few passageways from the square, then retrace your steps if necessary, without trying to cross private courtyards.

⏱ 60 min
2

You’ll pause in Mesta’s square in front of Megalos Taxiarchis. This large church stands on the site of the former central tower: the village’s defensive heart has changed its function. Go inside if it is open, wearing appropriate clothing, then have lunch before taking the winding road northwest.

⏱ 20 min
CarDrive to next stop: 53 km · 59min · Winding coastal road

Volissos

🛏️ Suggested base : Volissos
★★☆☆☆
1

Footsteps echo through the sloping lanes of Volissos, between Christos and Pythonas squares. The village rises in tiers beneath its fortress, with houses facing the western coast. After the drive from Mesta, save this walk for late afternoon and leave the climb to the castle for the following morning.

⏱ 35 min
2

Volissos Castle gives you a reason to climb before breakfast. Its six round towers outline an enclosure remodeled by the Genoese in the 15th century, above the houses and the coast. Set out early, wear closed-toe shoes, and stay on permitted access routes; exploring the ruins is unlike strolling through a landscaped garden.

⏱ 40 min
CarDrive to next stop: 36 km · 41min · Winding mountain road

Anavatos

🛏️ Suggested base : Chios · 11 km
★★★★☆
1

It is hard to separate Anavatos from its rocky outcrop: the tightly packed houses seem to extend the slope. This fortified village preserves the memory of the 1822 massacre; a single building housed a press, a school, a water reservoir, and a church. View the site from the open access routes and respect any barriers rather than trying to find a way through the ruins.

⏱ 45 min
CarDrive to next stop: 13 km · 18min · Winding mountain road

Nea Moni

🛏️ Suggested base : Chios · 7 km
★★★★★
1

If you arrive before the morning is over, Nea Moni gives you time to view its mosaics with gold backgrounds. This 11th-century monastery has been a World Heritage Site since 1990, along with Daphni and Hosios Loukas. Aim to enter around 11 AM: official opening hours are 9 AM–1 PM, except on Tuesdays.

⏱ 60 min
CarDrive to next stop: 12 km · 17min · Winding mountain road

Chios

🛏️ Suggested base : Chios
★★★★☆

Activities

You will finish with a walk along Aplotaria, Chios’s shopping street, before heading to the port. The town center lies between the waterfront and the castle: this final walk complements the first morning without repeating its visits. Above all, allow enough time to collect your luggage and reach your departure point.

⏱ 40 min
