Itineraries that are checked, dated and kept up to date

A OneRoadTrip itinerary isn't thrown together on the spot when you ask for it. It is built, checked and then approved before it reaches your screen.

  • Cross-checked information. Every visit and every activity is backed by web research. A piece of information is only kept if several sources confirm it.
  • Technology first, then a human eye. Artificial intelligence helps us research and write. Independent checks then review every claim, and our team approves the map, the texts and the photos.
  • Journeys calculated on real roads. Distances and driving times come from a road calculation, just like on a GPS. An inconsistent journey stops the itinerary from being published.
  • Translations checked one by one. Each version is compared against the original French.
  • A visible validation date. On every itinerary, a badge tells you when it was last validated.

The world moves fast. Opening hours, prices, roadworks, closed roads: we review our itineraries very often, but a museum can change its hours overnight. Before you set off, check the practical details of the places that matter most to you. And if you spot a mistake, the red ‘!’ on every itinerary lets us know in one click.

Want to know all about our method, how ratings are worked out or how distances are calculated? Our help page explains it in detail.

Five ways to find your trip

  • By criteria. Country, length, theme, distance: filter the catalogue and start from a ready-made itinerary.
  • Between two points. Enter a start and a finish, and the tool suggests the best places along the way.
  • From places. Pick your own stops on the map and put your route together.
  • On the world map. Spin the globe and let yourself be tempted.
  • With Lilia. Tell our assistant what you feel like doing, and she points you to the itineraries that fit.

Everything can be changed, down to the smallest detail

Once you've chosen your itinerary, you stay in control of everything:

  • Reorder the stops directly on the map
  • Add or remove a place, change the number of nights
  • Edit or expand the visits and activities at each stop
  • Ask for an automated opinion on how well your route hangs together
  • Paste in your booking emails to file them in your travel notebook
  • Keep track of your budget stop by stop
  • Print a polished travel notebook
  • Share your itinerary with your travel companions
  • Take it with you on your phone, even without a signal

Thousands of rated places

Every place in our database gets a score out of 10, based on objective criteria: historic sites, attractions, parks, UNESCO listings, the richness of its Wikipedia page, and the visits included in our itineraries.

Each place is then compared with its neighbours, with places in the same country, and then with the whole world. You can see at a glance what is unmissable, recommended or worth discovering.

Thanks to this database, OneRoadTrip suggests places worth the detour on any journey, not just the great classics.

Built-in bookings

From your itinerary, you can book hotels, activities, hire cars and flights with trusted partners: Booking.com, Viator, Trip.com and Qeeq. Everything comes together in a single dashboard, which you can open during your trip.

A completely free service

Every OneRoadTrip feature is free. No subscription, no paywall, no limit on creating or editing itineraries.

OneRoadTrip earns its income from the commissions our partners pay when you book through the site. It doesn't cost you any more: they share their margin.
